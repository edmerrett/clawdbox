Meet clawdbox: a desk companion for Claude Code.
Every time Claude reads a file, edits code, runs a command, or needs you, a hook fires. clawdbox turns those hooks into Clawd, animated live on a sixteen by sixteen Divoom panel, over Bluetooth.
Thirteen states, straight from the Claude Code stickers. A detective hat for reading. A skateboard for shell commands. A coffee when you have been away.
Open source. One command to install. clawdbox, on GitHub.
